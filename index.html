Pearl Calculator

The festival runs on Pearls. 2026 rate: 1 Pearl = €1.90 (≈ £1.63).

⇄
⇄

Top-ups come in multiples of €20 (€20 = 10.5 Pearls, €40 = 21, €60 = 31.5). Top up your bracelet or e-ticket online before the Bonus Pearls deadline (W1: 15 July, W2: 22 July — 23:59 CEST) and you receive Bonus Pearls: €100 → 2, €200 → 4, €300 → 7, €500 → 12, €700 → 17, €1,000 → 25. Unspent Pearls are refunded minus a small service fee.
